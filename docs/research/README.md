# Continuing factory and engineering-puzzle research

This is an ongoing design input, not a finite survey or a list of mechanics
already adopted by [DESIGN](../../DESIGN.md). `factory-yau.8` remains open after
the first batch, including when all currently seeded game studies are complete.
The PM checks continuity at each review; existing workers own and expand their
queues without waiting for individual dispatch.

The output we want is a growing collection of **interesting engineering puzzles**:
what the player is trying to achieve, the constraints that make invention useful,
meaningfully different solutions, their tradeoffs and failure modes, and an
original adaptation worth testing here. Research does not replace delivering the
stationary chapter or authorize a new game mechanic by itself.

## How a study proceeds

1. Claim an unoccupied game/coverage Bead and coordinate the per-game file in
   a separate canonical-Beads-linked worktree. Search related closed studies and
   pattern cards; record what is genuinely new.
2. Declare version, expansion/modpack, systems and progression range covered,
   evidence access and known gaps. Use the [study template](STUDY_TEMPLATE.md).
   A partial study stays open; do not mark the whole game thoroughly studied
   because one subsystem or marketing page was read.
3. Read primary sources where available: developer manuals/docs, official talks,
   design posts, source repositories or attributed demonstrations with timestamps.
   Community strategies/play records can supplement them; distinguish evidence
   from inference and disclose conflicts. Never invent first-hand play or claim
   enjoyment/popularity from an agent run.
4. Extract several concrete, varied puzzle patterns, including at least the
   most relevant ordinary, scaling and failure/recovery challenges within the
   declared scope. Show solution families or explain honestly when a puzzle is
   essentially single-solution. Do not copy protected levels, recipes or assets.
5. Review whether each pattern fits our physical, three-dimensional, mechanical
   game, gradual learning and eventual unattended automation. Separate a source
   game's actual rule from a proposed translation. Identify trivial shortcuts,
   compulsory upkeep, arbitrary waiting and ways the puzzle could destroy useful
   logistical meaning.
6. Arrange direct independent peer scrutiny of sources and adaptation reasoning.
   Preserve disagreements, rejected ideas and limits. Hand off exact source and
   catalogue links for normal root publication; authors do not self-approve.
7. Close the per-game leaf only when its declared scope is thoroughly covered,
   useful patterns and gaps are recorded, independent review is complete and
   root has recorded catalogue integration. Record scope, version/date, accepted
   note revision and reviewer in Beads. The recurring parent stays
   open and has an owned next target or useful synthesis/adaptation task.

Separate per-game paths permit parallel work without several writers owning the
same catalogue file. Coordinate new registry entries with PM; do not wait for an
index edit to do already-scoped research. Cross-link duplicate patterns instead
of rewriting them for every game. Game-note authors and peers can discover and
claim their next bounded study autonomously.

## Pattern cards: minimum useful content

Each card has a stable ID such as `factorio-01` and records:

- A clear goal, initial conditions and player verbs.
- Hard constraints versus soft incentives; geometry, orientation, quantities,
  timing, access, routing or throughput that actually create the decision.
- Why the decision could be satisfying, identified as design inference unless
  supported by attributed player evidence.
- Different solution families and their meaningful tradeoffs, or an explicit
  limitation if there is only one substantive approach.
- Failure, diagnosis and recovery; degenerate solutions and recurring-maintenance
  traps. Include the path to truly unattended operation where applicable.
- Teaching/prerequisites and likely progression placement. New processes should
  enable new products/capabilities, not only larger output numbers.
- An original mechanical/3D adaptation, prioritizing fixed/stationary factories;
  note whether internal motion, later controls or mobile factories are required.
- Engineering/state-ownership/performance risks, a small falsifiable prototype
  question, links to evidence and any related existing pattern/Bead.

Research cards progress from **candidate** to **evaluated** to **selected for an
experiment**, **rejected** or **deferred**, with reasons. Studying a game completely
does not mean accepting all its patterns. Only explicit design/integration
decisions promote an experiment into our requirements.

## Continuing operation and boundaries

Keep research useful and alive through owned queues, peer exchange and the
existing hourly PM review, not a one-off report marked done. When a round ends,
select a new gap, deeper clearly distinct scope, cross-game synthesis, or a
bounded adaptation experiment. Do not manufacture duplicate research or process
work merely to appear active.

The physical-character/stationary opening remains the implementation priority.
Use safe interface/review waits for source research; preparation may proceed
while an implementation is frozen. No new paid game purchases, large installs,
unbudgeted play sessions or extra worker agents are implied. Existing local
resource admission applies to any actual runtime experiments. Source browsing
and already permitted evidence are enough to begin; disclose what they cannot
establish.
