## Pattern <stable-game-id>-01: <engineering decision>

- **Goal and setup:** what must be achieved and what the player starts with.
- **Player verbs:** what can actually be built, changed, routed or controlled.
- **Constraints:** hard limits and soft rewards; what prevents the trivial answer.
- **Solution families:** distinct approaches and cost/space/complexity/robustness
  tradeoffs, or why there is only one meaningful family.
- **Appeal:** why it may invite invention; distinguish inference from evidence.
- **Failure and recovery:** what stalls, how it is diagnosed, how a solved design
  runs unattended; identify upkeep, waiting and degenerate shortcuts.
- **Teaching and progression:** prerequisite understanding, accessible first task,
  deeper variants, new product/capability versus simple output multiplication.
- **Adaptation here:** an original stationary, mechanical, 3D challenge, with any
  later expressive-control/mobile-factory dependencies labelled.
- **Engineering risks:** quantities/ownership, geometry, authority, continuation,
  simulation cost and relevant framework constraints; unknowns remain unknown.
- **Falsifier / smallest experiment:** the concrete observation that would show
  this is trivial, annoying, unexpressive or technically mismodelled.
- **Sources / related patterns:** precise evidence and deduplicated cross-links.
- **Disposition:** candidate / evaluated / experiment selected / rejected /
  deferred, with rationale. Not automatically accepted game design.
