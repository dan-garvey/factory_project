# First-chapter decision packet

Status: independent synthesis at `e33dcebc43a8c424ff7c8f3527e0f93bfdc4d727`.
Recommendation only: no chapter, part, resource safeguard, engine or implementation
is approved here. [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) owns requirements. The goal is a
small experience the user can try soon; no runtime or delivery date is established.

## Recommended first experience

Recommend softmetal **press A → channel-roll B → kit C**, with one small
player-built moving production arrangement and optional flat-roll A redesign:
scope B below. Start one lane before expansion; keep old A useful. Power,
feeding, motion and geometry must remain construction problems. This is the
smallest chapter candidate here that retains the central motion-invention test.
Scope A is a cheaper learning probe if the user explicitly accepts that reduced
goal; three fixed tool stations alone would not meet the first-chapter ambition.
Neither establishes a few-day estimate or waives eventual unattended operation.

## What the combined inputs establish, and where they remain conditional

1. **A new process has a purpose.** [Challenge](first-chapter-challenge.md):
   pressing makes plate A; opposed profiled rolls make guide channel B; kits C
   raise A demand. Flat rolls can adapt the B process for A. This is stronger
   than throughput-only progression. But if a valid old-head arrangement makes
   B, preserve that invention and revise the lesson, rather than forbid its
   recipe. Roll forming/material behavior has not been demonstrated.
2. **An advanced method must earn its cost.** [Layouts](first-chapter-layouts.md)
   compare indexed rotation, fixed-stroke gantry and flat rolls. Widening one
   gantry avoids growing its travel axis; widening one carousel grows its sweep.
   Repeated small carousels also permit roughly linear total growth. That control,
   plus actual support, feed, return and drive costs, prevents declaring gantries
   universally superior. Continuous roll handling is a plausible reward, not a
   measured capacity, footprint or power advantage. Keep old A practical for C.
3. **No programming does not mean a simple first build.** The challenge's small
   vocabulary grows into indexing/finite dwell, positive location, load support,
   stripping, one-item admission, sequential unload/load, floor closure and
   occupancy/backpressure retention. [Walkthrough](chapter-walkthrough.md) then
   needs phase-preserving stall/restart or a realizable rehome with retained work.
   An instant reversal or slower continuous rotation cannot replace dwell.
   These responsibilities cannot disappear inside a convenient all-purpose head.
   Teach complete functional heads, but leave feeding/transmission/motion for
   player arrangement; simplify the chapter if the shared mechanisms overwhelm it.
4. **Lasting source output is not whole-chain autonomy.** [Supply](chapter-supply.md)
   proposes a finite seam, then a milestone-gated fictional deep flow with an
   exploration shortcut and a harder base tap. Hand power is bootstrap; the
   proposed waterwheel removes ongoing cranking. Receiver capacity, physical
   haulage, forming, packing/unpacking and replacement construction parts all
   still need working handling. Named future bundlers/parts benches are not proof.
   A powered stationary cutter does not survey and replace exhausted finite sites.
5. **Depletion safety depends on a new restriction.** The supply argument needs
   a reusable starter kit and a recoverable, unspendable construction reserve.
   Its illustrative six blank-equivalents become 4 A + 2 B for the base tap; they
   are not the commission budget. Three illustrative kits need 42 further blanks,
   plus separate check/build costs. Consuming prerequisite checks requires a
   larger reserve. Destruction, shipment, lost access, late arrivals and blocked
   source access can invalidate the argument. Making B after initial extraction
   may unlock supply; exhaustion must neither grant it nor be required for it.
   Rejecting reserve protection requires another resource-independent rebuild
   route. More finite seams alone only postpone a possible permanent dead end.
6. **Local availability is different from information.** [Construction supply](construction-supply.md)
   distinguishes a BOM/location list, a physically stocked worksite cache and
   later transported requests. Flow production does not deliver construction
   parts to the player. Trace source → carried/cache stock → placed part once;
   stale counts cannot fund placement. Blocked shipments stay with their actual
   holder, and cancellation does not recreate stock. Shared goods do not settle
   discovery, withdrawal, building permission or personal learning/quest credit.

The combined chapter remains conditional on these joins. Treating the full
bundle as the novice start adds a large untested learning burden; naming each
mechanism does not make the sequence accessible or dependable.

## Two coherent demo scopes to choose between

**A — Reduced learning probe.** At a small designed site, manually
cut finite blanks and make A under a visibly working supported press. Build the
paired roll shafts, direction-changing gears and supported input/output for B.
Offer C's increased A demand and the optional flat-roll A arrangement; retaining
and manually tending the old press remains a valid completion path. Local kit
packing is explicitly manual in this demo. Include one inspectable rotate/zoom
explanation, real gravity/tool-face/clearance consequences and actual stock
retrieval. Continuous roll feed still needs retained output/backpressure; it is
not a magic feeder. Its smaller phase burden is a hypothesis to test. This can
exercise cog routing and real gravity/tool clearance, but has no player-built
moving production arrangement. The press's internal ram does not supply that
missing witness. Motion invention/carrier scaling remain untested; this may
feel like recipe assembly. Do not call A a complete first-chapter experience.

Use declared finite fixture stock adequate for the accepted session bill; any
between-run reset is disclosed, with no refill during a run. This tests removing
forming/handling work and choosing a new method, not lasting supply, general
recovery or a depletion-safe campaign. The flow/reserve project remains a
separate optional extension requiring decision 3. A is a deliberately bounded
probe, not evidence that the intended chapter systems work together.

**B — Compact carrier chapter (recommended, conditional).** Add buildable R/G
handling to A: common cycle drive, dwell, support/stripper/gates, retained occupancy
and restart. The player starts one lane; comparative growth/fault runs can be
separate acceptance runs rather than compulsory tutorial steps.
The optional press-tending project becomes a substantial construction lesson;
quest credit still does not demand automation. Actual player construction of a
working moving arrangement is required evidence for the chapter claim, even
when that project is optional for quest completion. Compare an added G
lane against widened R and repeated small R, then offer the flat-roll adaptation.
Exercise whole-unit isolation and receiver-draining recovery with the disabled
takeaway control. Include the supply extension only if separately chosen.
This better tests the original geometric ambition but carries more mechanism,
teaching and implementation risk. Cutting dwell or resetting away a fault would
make it an incomplete version of B, not a successful simpler implementation.

The timber alternative is still available as a content direction, but needs its
own clamping/feed, chips, verticality and lasting-supply work. It is not a ready
drop-in substitute for either scope's softmetal accounting.

## Five user choices; recipe tuning can wait

1. **Content:** try softmetal plates/channels/component kits, or request another
   production problem? Recommend trying softmetal with reusable tooling and no
   scrap/wear/fuel/binder chain in this slice. Those omissions are fictional
   process assumptions, not permission to hide future consumables or waste.
2. **First experience:** B's motion chapter, or explicitly reduce the goal to A's
   learning probe? Recommend a small B, with handling improvement as the proposed
   roll-A reward and its phase/support/recovery responsibilities visible.
   Neither choice approves a whole first release or requires one copied layout.
3. **Supply extension:** trial the deep-flow premise, exposed shortcut/base bore,
   initial-extraction-plus-B availability and recoverable reserve principle;
   keep the disclosed finite fixture only; or revise supply before a campaign?
   Recommend the finite fixture for the first narrow demo. A campaign proposal
   still needs the accepted safeguard or equivalent rebuild path, with a real
   bill and shared-world access rules. Do not ask the user to bless six forever.
4. **Construction access:** carried stock plus BOM/location help, or also a
   physically moved/stocked worksite toolbox? Recommend the toolbox with actual
   source/cache interaction. No invisible withdrawal radius or delivery network
   is assumed; observe missing-part trips and moving/replenishing it at a second
   worksite. Later physical receiving must eventually remove recurring errands.
5. **Completion and credit:** non-consuming inventory confirmation, or a
   consuming check with a revised bootstrap bill? Recommend non-consuming checks.
   Separately choose whether this demo ends with kits ready in inventory or
   includes a physical dispatch that removes only delivered goods. Declare trial
   stock-access and participant-credit rules for any two-client use. A shared
   production pile cannot silently select the intended individual/team/world
   progression model; that remains the separate ownership decision.

core/scene trial. Its small-download/manual-binding route is not measured rapid
iteration, a chapter implementation or production adoption. Permission `.3.2`,
production choice `.3.5` and playable decisions are separate. If a different
route is preferred, revise the bounded packet; do not silently substitute it.

[Save/resume](save-resume-contract.md) requires continuation with real identities,
item locations, held work, phase and request dispositions, not just a successful
load. Neither the trial nor these papers establish that behavior. No API,
storage framework, engine choice or expanded experiment budget follows here.

The [common witness](prototype-witness.md) still needs rotary/gantry/train-like
motion, real player/collision joins, moving harvest/transfers, headless authority,
two-client conflicts/correction and save/rejoin. Late precision-control probes
stay developer risk tests; they do not become early progression requirements.
Keep those risks early even if A reduces the first teaching session. Do not
waive existing gates to claim a few-day delivery; the PM must expose any proposed
change in evidence order. Intended trains, moving factories/passengers, multiple
teams/independent learning, autonomous finite acquisition, later controls,
fluids/gases, terrain progression and campaign endings all retain later scope.

Before accepting either demo, run the actual build/diagnose/expand session:
account for real inputs/held work/output; retain and correct direction/capacity
faults; show support/height changing operation; compare old/new A at equivalent
useful output including feed/access costs; record hints, retrieval trips and
