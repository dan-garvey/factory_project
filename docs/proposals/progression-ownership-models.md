# Progression, shared work and delivery credit

Status: comparison proposal for `factory-yau.2.2`, based on `b2a1fcf`. No model,
consumption rule, access system or campaign implementation is selected. The ten
[accepted learning scenarios](learning-pace-scenarios.md) are the comparison
cases; their acceptance did not choose how progress is shared.

## Requirements and terms

[DESIGN](../../DESIGN.md#multiplayer-and-progression-ownership) requires several
cooperative teams and an option to keep one's own progression and learning pace.
It leaves individual, team and world sharing open. [Ordinary checks](../../DESIGN.md#quest-completion)
start with obtaining a product in inventory and manually confirming it; they do
not prove production or automation. Nonconsumption remains a campaign proposal.
[Off-site commissions and the two endings](../../DESIGN.md#campaign-and-manager)
are distinct: the true ending requires throughput of several end-game recipes,
whose products, rates and measurement remain unchosen.

Here a **charter** means the person or group to whose campaign record an accepted
check or commission belongs. It is a comparison term, not a proposed UI name,
account system or storage format. Learning records, recipe knowledge, permission
to operate somebody else's machine, physical custody and campaign credit are
different facts. A team label alone grants none of the other four.

Every candidate keeps one shared physical world. Deposits stay depleted, terrain
stays changed, goods have actual holders, and an accepted shipment happens once.
Personal messages may be deferred; an observed factory or world event cannot be
made unseen. The options below promise different kinds of independence, rather
than claiming to preserve an untouched beginner world.

## Three candidate arrangements

| State or event | A: personal charters | B: opt-in team charters | C: personal learning, world campaign |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lessons and ordinary confirmation | Individual offered lessons and confirmations. Gifted goods qualify for an offered inventory check after the recipient confirms; no proof of manufacture is implied. | Individual study/confirmation records remain available. The first qualifying member confirmation also completes the charter's offered check; it does not mark every member's personal study complete. | Individual offered lessons and confirmations, as in A; completing the world campaign does not complete them. |
| Recipe/build capability | Individual milestones unlock that person's recipes. Inspection and operation of an existing machine require the holder's permission, not automatic lesson completion. | The chosen campaign charter supplies build entitlements. Joining its advanced track grants those entitlements immediately; personal study may still start earlier. Operating existing equipment is a separate permission. | Individual recipe milestones, as in A. World shipment progress alone grants no personal recipe in this comparison. |
| Stock, machines and access | World custody with declared individual/team permissions; personal recipe knowledge does not authorize withdrawal or dismantling. | The same physical rule. Working with T need not require joining T's campaign charter. | The same physical rule. Contributing to a world project does not grant access to another team's stock. |
| Commission and contribution credit | Orders belong to individuals. A joint cargo declares how many actual units serve each person's order. Contributions may be acknowledged separately. | Orders belong to campaign charters, commonly T and U. A joint cargo declares each charter's allocation. Member recognition does not create extra shipped units. | Orders belong to the world project. T/U contribution receipts may record their declared allocation, while the world receives the actual total once. Team rankings are not implied. |
| Story occurrence and disclosure | World facts happen once. Personal campaign eligibility/recognition and seen/deferred messages belong to each person; a late player may receive a recap of an event that already happened. | World facts happen once; charter milestones determine campaign eligibility/recognition. Seen/deferred messages remain personal. | Campaign milestones and irreversible events advance for the world. Personal messages can wait, but personal campaign timing cannot be promised. |
| Semi-ending and true-ending record | Personal campaign recognition and a personal qualifying throughput record. Shared machinery can serve explicitly assigned personal targets. | Charter campaign recognition and qualifying throughput record. Membership alone does not create another measured output. | World campaign recognition and qualifying throughput record. Personal study remains available afterward; world completion is not personal proof of mastery. |

These arrangements have different costs. A preserves independent campaign
records but may demand repetitive parallel orders from one factory; it cannot
make the megaproject occur separately in each person's physical timeline. B
makes joint projects straightforward, but shared advancement must be an explicit
choice. A newcomer must be able to keep a solo campaign charter while obtaining
permission to work with T. This separates work access from campaign membership
and adds explanation and permission costs. Without that alternative B does not
provide independent capability progression inside an advanced group.

C avoids parallel campaign demands and gives the shared world a coherent project
history. It preserves personal lessons and recipe progression, but fails a promise
of independent campaign/reveal timing: another team can finish the world project
while N is away. It is viable only if that narrower meaning of "own progression"
is acceptable. A single world track that also auto-completes every person's
lessons and recipes does not meet the independent-progress requirement.

## Checks, shipping and throughput are separate

For comparison, all three use a nonconsuming ordinary check. The authoritative
decision observes the submitter's qualifying inventory and offered milestone;
manual confirmation changes the declared owner's credit once and moves no goods.
There is no pooled remote-inventory check. Another eligible person may later
receive the same goods and confirm their own check. That is sequential custody
and shared learning, not two copies of the goods or repeated credit for a retry.

| Consumption alternative, still a proposal | Physical/accounting consequence and tradeoff |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary check retains goods; off-site project uses delivered goods | A check changes no custody. Dispatch moves goods through an actual route to the off-site holder; project use then removes them from available stock. This supports reuse for learning but still requires replacement production for commissions. |
| Ordinary check retains goods; a commission returns its cargo | The return requires a physical route and explicit acceptance/credit terms. Returning one cargo cannot prove repeated new production. This fits a loan or inspection task more naturally than a project needing material. |
| Ordinary check consumes its required goods as well as shipping | The check needs an explicit sink and increases total demand. Re-evaluate bootstrap and later source access before accepting it; the same units cannot also be shipped. This changes the nonconsumption proposal. |

The traces below stop at **off-site custody**; they do not choose whether the
project consumes those goods or might return them. Loading a dock, accepting a
dispatch and completing the recipient's order must be distinguishable. A pending
or refused dispatch leaves goods with their actual local/in-transit holder;
cancellation cannot recreate them at the source.

The true-ending counter has a different basis: actual eligible recipe production
over the eventual measurement window, retaining one result per required recipe.
An inventory confirmation, stockpile unloading burst or shipment count alone is
insufficient. For comparison, a qualifying production event is allocated once to
the personal, charter or world record named above. Shared recognition may name
many people; it cannot multiply measured physical output. Products, rates,
simultaneity/window rules, meter placement and treatment of ownership changes are
future choices. None of these papers establishes a working throughput meter or
replaces ordinary checks with sustained-automation tests.

## Apply the accepted learning scenarios

N is the newcomer, V the experienced player, and T/U cooperative teams. B below
assumes N explicitly chose T's campaign charter; its solo-charter alternative
keeps N's campaign separate while allowing declared cooperative work access.
Shared physical changes in the second column apply to all three candidates.

| Case | Physical result and remaining practice opportunity | A | B | C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LP-join | T's advanced factory remains present. N needs a permitted place and actual inputs for a basic chain; joining cannot grant either by implication. | N starts offered lessons/recipes and campaign orders independently. T's prior progress creates no N credit. | T's existing campaign entitlements become available immediately; N's earlier study remains uncompleted. A solo charter is required to decline that shared advancement. | N's lessons/recipes start independently, while world orders may already be complete. Earlier work needs a personal lesson rather than a second world commission. |
| LP-gift | A product and a complete machine physically move from V to N. No new production occurs. | An offered manual inventory check may give N credit. Machine use and the ability to construct another machine remain separate. | N can confirm personal study; T's check changes only if still offered/incomplete. T's existing build entitlements do not prove N learned the process. | N can confirm personal study/recipe progress; world commission and throughput records stay unchanged. |
| LP-machine | Using T's advanced machine requires permission and leaves that machine in the world. An earlier method needs its own reachable inputs/work area. | Use does not auto-complete N's lesson or grant a new recipe. N may choose the old route. | Use grants no personal study credit; T's shared recipes may already remove practical constraints. Keeping the old lesson meaningful requires an available simpler arrangement. | Use does not complete N's lesson; world milestones are irrelevant to this individual confirmation. |
| LP-reveal | A real off-site shipment changes its declared recipient's record. N may miss or defer the message, not undo the shipment or visible evidence. | N's own order/milestone is unchanged unless named as a beneficiary. World facts may still reveal something. | T advances, U does not unless allocated cargo. N can defer the message, but cannot delay the charter event. | The world campaign advances for everyone. N's message/lesson waits; independent campaign timing is not protected. |
| LP-deposit | V's depleted seam stays empty. Each model needs a reachable permitted alternative finite source or a genuinely available harder supply route, including its bootstrap inputs. | N's personal recipe gates may block the alternate route even when V knows it. Resolve access/bootstrap explicitly. | T may know the route, but a solo charter may not. Cooperation must not require surrendering the independent track. | World progress supplies no personal recipe or input automatically. A completed campaign does not solve the missing beginner source. |
| LP-terrain | T's excavation persists. A usable site and technology-safe route must exist; a checklist is not a place to build. | Personal build/traversal rights must leave N an actual route. Shared help is optional, not a terrain reset. | T's shared entitlements may open access; a solo charter still needs a supported route. Permission to cross T's site remains separate. | Individual gates remain relevant despite advanced world progress. Another team's excavation cannot be masked as private beginner terrain. |
| LP-joint | N gathers, V processes, and T/U load one cargo. The accepted dispatch allocates actual units once, as traced below. | Named individual orders receive their allocated amounts; helpers receive no automatic duplicate order completion. | Named charters receive allocations; member study checks remain separate. | The world receives the total; declared T/U contributions partition that total, not add to it. |
| LP-switch | N leaves T for U, or leaves without joining another team, with goods, a pending action and old machine permission. Already accepted work retains its original beneficiary; current permissions govern new admissions. | N keeps personal learning/credit with or without a new team. T's machine access may end; carried goods still need explicit custody/entitlement treatment. | Personal study survives. A new campaign charter supplies its entitlements; without one, resume N's prior solo charter or start a fresh solo charter if none exists. T's accepted credit stays in T. | N keeps personal learning/recipes with or without a new team. World campaign history stays; T/U contribution attribution does not follow N retroactively. |
| LP-return | The server may have operated, depleted inputs and changed terrain. A recap and a feasible practice route are different needs. | N's records survive; the world is current, not restored to N's departure. | Personal study survives while T's campaign may have advanced. A solo charter avoids automatic campaign advancement, not world changes. | N's records survive and world campaign progress is current, possibly complete. The lost chance to witness a shared event cannot be promised back. |
| LP-reconnect | Reconcile actual request disposition, current custody and current permissions before retrying. An uncertain client response is not an accepted shipment. | Recover the named individual's accepted credit without awarding it again. | Recover the originally named charter's credit, even if N has since switched. | Recover the one world dispatch and original contribution allocation; do not add a second global contribution. |

For B, leaving without a new campaign charter resumes the prior solo charter's
own progress and build entitlements, or starts a baseline solo charter if none
exists. Personal study persists, but T's commission credit and team-granted
entitlements are not copied into that solo record; T's records remain intact.
This is the proposed no-team continuation, not automatic campaign migration.
In every model, departure leaves carried, docked and in-transit goods with their
actual holders. Accepted work keeps its original beneficiary; still-unadmitted
work rechecks current permission and cannot silently retarget to a solo charter.

No ownership model alone passes LP-deposit/terrain/return. A proposed supply route
must actually be available to the late-arriving player under that model's gates,
with its inputs, space and transport accounted for. The
[supply](chapter-supply.md) and [construction-access](construction-supply.md)
papers offer candidates, not a guaranteed protected reserve or fresh private
site. Permanent depletion never triggers a free lasting-source unlock.

## One continuous custody and credit trace

These quantities and letter labels are paper examples, not chapter content or
requirements for per-item provenance. J is a member of U. N is studying early
work while cooperating with V in T's existing advanced factory.

1. N extracts four finite blanks and V processes them into four plates a–d.
   J supplies two plates e–f from U's actual production. At this cut N carries
   a/b, V carries c/d and J carries e/f: six plates, no plates still at source.
   Those finite inputs are removed once; no source replenishes because of credit.
2. N confirms an offered two-plate study check using a/b. A/C credit N; in B N's
   study changes, while T's already completed earlier check stays completed.
   Custody remains 2 + 2 + 2. Gifting a/b to another eligible learner later could
   support a separate manual check, but cannot count as new production.
3. All six plates are physically loaded into one dispatch. Carried stock becomes
   zero and cargo becomes six. A declares personal order allocations N2/V2/J2;
   B declares charter orders T4/U2; C declares one world order6 with T4/U2
   contribution allocation. These are alternative credit records for the SAME
   cargo, not simultaneous claims or extra physical plates. Loading has not yet
   completed any order.
4. After physical transit, the recipient handoff commits the dispatch once and
   transfers the six units to the off-site holder. This is completion of the
   physical handoff, not admission or queuing of its initiating command.
   Local/cargo stock becomes zero; off-site custody becomes six. Award
   exactly the declared allocations. N disconnects before seeing the reply.
   No true-ending meter was armed, so these ordinary products/shipments add no
   true-ending throughput credit. Project consumption remains unchosen.
5. N switches work access to U and, in B, explicitly chooses U's campaign charter
   before reconnecting. For this trace T revokes permission for new machine-use
   requests. Reconciliation recovers the
   accepted original allocation: A keeps N's personal credit; B keeps T4/U2;
   C keeps world6 and the original contributions. It does not recast the request
   under N's new membership or ship six more. A still-unadmitted T request would
   instead recheck permission and, if refused, leave cargo at its actual holder.
6. N's personal study persists in every model. A/C retain N's personal recipes;
   B retains learned information but build entitlement follows the explicitly
   chosen charter. Moving to a less advanced charter can therefore remove an
   entitlement without erasing knowledge, a significant cooperation cost to
   review. N can choose an offered earlier lesson, but nobody can promise new
   practice stock from the six plates now off-site. An actual alternative source
   is still required; another team can offer help that N may decline.

The [stationary disposition contract](stationary-authority-contract.md#uncertain-requests-at-disconnect-and-restore)
supplies the accepted/rejected/pending/unresolved distinction. This trace adds no
network retry guarantee, team implementation, account identity or save schema.
The current opening's P/Q fixture is not a selection of campaign membership.

## Tradeoffs for independent review and a later scoped choice

Review A for repeated delivery work and conflicting personal recognition of one
world event. Review B for the cost of keeping work permissions and campaign
membership separate, entitlement loss at a team change, and whether declining
shared advancement remains practical. Review C for the narrower independence
promise and the permanent loss of an opportunity to witness a world milestone.
Across all three, test an exhausted beginner site before claiming that a personal
lesson is still playable; reject a permanently unavailable prerequisite.

For a future campaign trial, B gives a useful comparison starting point when
players want a shared project but may decline its advancement; A is the stronger
alternative when independent campaign credit is required. C is a deliberate
world-campaign alternative, not the presumed default. This is a proposed trial
order, not adoption. The next scoped choice should say which independence is
promised, how charter/work access relates, what a gift qualifies, who receives
joint dispatch credit, and what happens to bootstrap goods under consumption.
True-ending metering needs a separate concrete measurement contract.

`factory-yau.2.3` records an actual revisable choice by the delegated design owner
or direct user direction. The current [delegation](../../DESIGN.md#delegated-defaults-for-the-basic-opening-demo)
does not require routine permission cards; comparison or review completion still
does not itself make a choice. The accepted opening keeps its nonconsuming
four-plate personal session confirmation, with no campaign unlock or off-site
commission. This packet adds no first-playable prerequisite and claims no game,
learning, fun, persistence or multiplayer performance evidence.
