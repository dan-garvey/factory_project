# Learning at different paces in one shared world

Status: scenario proposal for `factory-yau.2.1`, based on `d64217b`. No progression,
access, credit, consumption, territory or narrative-sharing model is selected.
These are paper cases for comparing models in `.2.2`, not runtime evidence or
permission to implement persistent progression.

## Source and staging

[DESIGN multiplayer ownership](../../DESIGN.md#multiplayer-and-progression-ownership)
requires multiple cooperative teams and an option to retain one's own progression
and learning pace; sharing within teams remains open. [Review finding 3](../../DESIGN_REVIEW.md#prioritized-findings)
identifies the gap: personal unlock records cannot undo another player's machinery,
depletion, terrain changes or revelations in a shared physical world.
[Quest completion](../../DESIGN.md#quest-completion) starts from inventory plus
manual confirmation; nonconsumption is still a proposal. Off-site commissions
and the true ending's throughput condition need distinct treatment.

The accepted opening remains collector → physical transport → processor → product.
Full team/story progression and these later cases are not new prerequisites for
first feedback. An opening fixture needs only the roles and rules it exercises;
it cannot be presented as resolving this packet. See the
[current chapter decision packet](first-chapter-decision-packet.md).
If a later technical demo borrows a case, label its permissions and world setup
as demo-only assumptions; doing so neither selects a model nor adds an opening gate.

## What might “own learning pace” protect?

The user has not yet chosen among these different promises:

| Possible protection | Observable question; limit in a shared world |
| --- | --- |
| Instruction and credit | Can a newcomer choose their next lesson and confirm their own achievement, even if the team has finished it? This alone does not preserve a fresh production problem. |
| Opportunity to practise | Can they still obtain inputs and build a basic working chain? Existing supplies, exhausted sites or altered terrain can make a nominally available lesson trivial or impossible. |
| Capability access | Can they choose when to use advanced goods/machinery? Permission to inspect, operate, construct and claim credit may differ; a personal unlock does not remove a machine from the world. |
| Discovery and story pace | Can they defer personal messages/explanations? Delayed text cannot hide everything they witness another player building or doing. |

`.2.2` should compare which promises a model delivers and which it cannot.
Do not call personal quest counters alone complete independent progression.

## Concrete comparison scenarios

Use a newcomer **N**, an experienced player **V**, their current team **T**, and
another cooperative team **U**. Names and example goods are fixture labels.
For each case, declare candidate permissions and state ownership before walking
it; the questions below deliberately leave their answers open.

| Case and starting change | What the comparison must expose |
| --- | --- |
| LP-join: N joins T after V has automated several processes. N wants to place their first collector and processor. | Which lessons/checks remain available? Does joining grant capabilities, credit, both, or neither? Can N practise the basic chain without dismantling V's factory? Compare optional shared help with a protected practice opportunity; neither is an adopted rule. |
| LP-gift: V hands N an advanced product and a complete advanced machine. | Trace actual custody. Is possession sufficient for an ordinary inventory check after N confirms it? Can N operate or place the machine before learning its process? Separate gifting, usable capability, chapter credit and proof of production; a gift is not evidence of automation. |
| LP-machine: N uses T's advanced shared machine, then tries its earlier manual process. | Does use automatically advance instruction/unlocks? Can N inspect a simple explanation and try the old method voluntarily? Test build/use/inspection permissions separately. Cosmetic hiding cannot make an operating shared machine physically absent. |
| LP-reveal: T fulfils an off-site commission while N has deferred its manager conversation. U has reached another milestone. | Which events change T, U or world progress, and which messages has N seen? Can N defer/replay a message without undoing the actual shipment? Record spoilers from visible machines and other players separately from controllable message disclosure. No on-site megastructure is implied. |
| LP-deposit: V exhausts the finite deposit nearest N's intended starting site before N learns extraction. | The deposit stays exhausted. What reachable, permitted source or harder in-base route still allows N to learn and bootstrap? Compare alternate sites, coordinated reserves or a different supply route as proposals; personal progress must not be mistaken for replenished ore. Reject a model with a permanently exhausted required prerequisite and no route. |
| LP-terrain: T excavates the beginner work area and changes its access route; a remaining resource lies beyond a technology-gated region. | Does N have a usable site and safe permitted route, or merely an uncompleted quest? Consider adapting the shared site versus a distinct practice site, including their space/supply costs. Neither private terrain resets nor free traversal technology is assumed. |
| LP-joint: N gathers material, V processes it, and T and U both contribute to one dispatch. | Identify the actual products/cargo and accepted shipment, then ask who gains item-check, contribution, commission, story and ending credit. Show ordinary manual confirmation separately. If consumption is chosen, account for it once; credit sharing cannot duplicate physical goods. Contribution tracking is a policy question, not a required provenance framework. |
| LP-switch: N leaves T for U with carried goods, a pending delivery and permission to use a T-owned machine. | Which learned records persist, which capabilities/credits transfer, and when do access changes apply? Goods and machinery retain explicit physical holders. Compare treatment of already accepted versus pending work; team change cannot silently clone stock or erase a live delivery. Include leaving without joining another team. |
| LP-return: N is offline while T advances, sources deplete and the factory keeps operating. | On return, distinguish N's earlier learning record from the current world. Can N choose a recap or earlier lesson? A personal backlog neither rolls back terrain nor guarantees untouched starting resources. Explain what practice remains possible now. |
| LP-reconnect: N confirms a check or requests a shipment, disconnects before seeing the response, then reconnects after V advances the factory. | Establish whether the request was accepted, rejected, pending or unresolved under the trial contract before offering retry. Reconcile current custody and credited outcome; no duplicated goods, repeated credit from a retry or client-only success. Identity/order/transport mechanisms remain unspecified. |

Depletion and terrain cases must include the late-arriving player, not just a
successful original team's supply route. A lasting source unlocks through
technology, not because a deposit empties. The
[supply proposal](chapter-supply.md) offers unaccepted candidate routes; it does
not guarantee a reserved newcomer deposit or accessible source for every team.

## Candidate state owners to distinguish

This is a comparison inventory, not a storage schema. Physical facts and the
credit attached to them need not have the same owner.

| Candidate state | Individual, team and world possibilities to compare |
| --- | --- |
| Lessons offered/completed; explanation/message seen or deferred | Individual records can preserve instruction order; team/world milestones may make an explanation relevant. Sharing completion must be a declared policy. |
| Technology known; rights to build, operate or traverse | Compare personal versus team capability and world-enabled opportunities. Knowing a recipe, owning an item and being authorized to use it are distinct. |
| Items, containers, machines, deposits and terrain | Shared-world physical state with explicit holders/access. Entitlements or site assignments can be personal/team policies; they cannot create different authoritative quantities for different viewers. |
| Ordinary inventory confirmation | Compare who submits, whose inventory qualifies, whose credit changes and whether checking consumes anything. Nonconsuming checks and credit for gifted goods stay open. |
| Dispatch and contribution records | Separate actual physical shipment from individual/team contribution and commission credit. A world event need not complete every team's commission. |
| Chapter/mystery milestones and ending progress | Compare individual/team/world scope for event occurrence, disclosure and credit separately. True-ending throughput is not an ordinary check or automatic proof attributed to every online player. |
| Membership/access and outstanding requests | Preserve the actor/context of accepted work while evaluating current permissions for new work. Save/rejoin must reconcile the chosen disposition; a membership label alone is insufficient. |

Access and custody should remain consistent with the
[construction-supply alternatives](construction-supply.md). Knowing another
team's stock location is not permission or a delivery connection. Neither shared
credit nor a personal lesson supplies remote inventory withdrawal.
Use the [stationary authority contract](stationary-authority-contract.md#uncertain-requests-at-disconnect-and-restore)
to distinguish request dispositions; this proposal adds no transport guarantee.

## Evidence and next decision

For each proposed model, `.2.2` should fill one short record per case:

- Before/after physical holders, site/resources and the actual accepted event.
- N's available lessons, capabilities, credit and messages; T/U/world records
  that change, including records that stay unchanged.
- The concrete route N can still take, the help they may decline, and the
  unavoidable shared-world effects the model does not protect them from.
- A conflicting action or disconnect, its declared disposition, and any extra
  infrastructure/territory/reservation cost the model introduces.

A policy is not viable merely because all counters have somewhere to live.
Trace LP-join → LP-gift → LP-machine → LP-joint → LP-switch → LP-reconnect
through the same people and stock, then LP-deposit/terrain/return through the
same changed world. Independent critique should expose contradiction between
promised learning freedom, actual available work and cooperative access.

`.2.2` owns the alternative models and their tradeoffs; `.2.3` records actual
user decisions. Before adopting a model, ask what “own pace” promises, what an
advanced gift permits, who receives joint-work credit and what a newcomer can
still practise after shared-world change. Open choices remain open; neither
this scenario list nor its review grants a common unlock track or consumption
rule. No gameplay, fun, recovery or multiplayer performance has been tested.
