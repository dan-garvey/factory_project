# Ownership for a stationary delivery experiment

Status: source-reviewed experimental contract for `factory-yau.2.3`, under the
user's delegation of reversible design choices. This document implements no
progression, permission or delivery code and selects no permanent campaign policy.

The next optional commission experiment will keep learning and recipe entitlement
personal, while allowing an explicit choice of a solo or shared campaign record.
Physical stock, machines, deposits and terrain remain in one authoritative world.
This combines the personal capability rule and optional shared commission records
from the [ownership comparison](../proposals/progression-ownership-models.md).
It selects an experiment rather than a permanent campaign policy.

The existing opening keeps its four-plate, nonconsuming session confirmation.
An off-site delivery, team system or technology unlock is not a new requirement
for that demo. The [accepted learning scenarios](../proposals/learning-pace-scenarios.md)
are the cases below; their earlier acceptance did not select this contract.

## Chosen owners and admission rules

A **campaign record** identifies the person or voluntarily joined group whose
offered commission receives credit. The comparison calls this a charter. It is
separate from a work team, machine permission and the inventory holding an item.
A player starts with a solo campaign record and can keep it while working with
an advanced team. Joining a work team does not silently select its campaign.

| Fact | Owner and experimental rule |
| --- | --- |
| Lessons and explanations | The learner owns offered/completed checks and seen/deferred messages. Team progress never marks that learner's study complete. Earlier explanations remain available. |
| Recipe entitlement | Any entitlement exercised by this experiment belongs to the learner. Joining or leaving a campaign does not copy or revoke personal recipes. Which later checks grant which recipes is outside this slice. |
| Ordinary inventory confirmation | The learner manually confirms an offered check against their own actual inventory. It consumes nothing and credits that learner once. A gift can qualify; possession is not proof of manufacturing or automation. |
| Machine and storage access | The authoritative holder's permission governs inspection, operation and withdrawal. Recipe knowledge, campaign membership, a distant camera or another team's stock location supplies no permission or physical delivery. |
| Construction | The actor needs the applicable personal recipe entitlement, permission, actual parts and the existing authoritative physical reach. Operating a permitted existing machine does not grant its recipe. |
| Commission | An offered order belongs to its named campaign record. A cargo declares a fixed allocation among eligible records before dispatch. The actual recipient handoff credits those allocations once. |
| Material use | An accepted commission consumes its actual cargo into the off-site project's terminal use record. Loading and pending transit do not consume it or complete the order. Ordinary inventory checks remain nonconsuming. |
| Story | A physical event happens once in the shared world. Campaign recognition follows the credited record; each person controls their own messages. This does not hide visible machines or undo a shipment. |

An explicit campaign opt-in can expose an advanced group's existing campaign
milestones and recognition immediately. It still completes no personal lesson or
recipe check. Declining that opt-in preserves the solo record, rather than
requiring a second server or leaving the cooperative work team.

This hybrid avoids recipe loss when changing teams and reduces compulsory
duplicate deliveries for players who want one shared project. It costs another
visible distinction: working together and sharing campaign advancement are
different choices. Shared campaign credit is not evidence that every member
learned the process. A later trial should challenge whether players understand
that distinction before expanding it into the full campaign.

## Physical dispatch, credit and uncertain requests

Only goods actually present in the dispatch's authorized local holder can be
loaded. The experiment uses an ordinary physical route to an off-site recipient,
not an inventory deduction at a distance. The transport form and world boundary
are not selected here; they need a separate bounded implementation.

1. Declare the source holder, quantities, order and beneficiary allocation. The
   allocations must sum to the cargo quantity. Permission and order eligibility
   are checked authoritatively; a campaign label cannot create missing goods.
2. Loading transfers those units to the cargo holder. It grants no commission
   credit. In transit the same units remain accounted for, including while a
   caller is disconnected or a response is pending.
3. At the actual recipient handoff, recheck the frozen allocation, each order's
   remaining demand and current authority. Refuse the whole handoff if an
   allocation no longer qualifies; keep the cargo rather than silently choosing
   another beneficiary or partially completing the transfer. On acceptance,
   commit one terminal project-use record and its declared
   credit together. Available goods become project-used goods once, rather than
   remaining available for another shipment.
4. A refused or not-yet-admitted handoff leaves the goods with their actual holder.
   Cancellation before acceptance requires an ordinary return or unload route;
   it cannot recreate the cargo at its origin. There is no refund after accepted
   project use in this experiment.

For the example's plates, local stored stock plus carried stock plus in-transit
stock plus committed project use equals the total plates produced or otherwise
explicitly introduced. These holders are disjoint. Other recipes need their own
declared input/output accounting. Recognition may name several helpers, but
allocated commission quantities cannot exceed the actual units used. No universal per-item provenance
scheme, contribution ranking or extra quantity owner is required by this choice.

An accepted request retains its original actor, context, order, beneficiary and
outcome. A player may change work team or campaign while a handoff is pending or
unresolved. That membership change neither retries the handoff nor determines its
earlier disposition. Before retrying the handoff or starting a replacement using
the same cargo, reconcile its accepted, rejected, pending or unresolved disposition
under the
[existing authority contract](../proposals/stationary-authority-contract.md#uncertain-requests-at-disconnect-and-restore).
Replaying its identity cannot ship again or change the credited campaign. A
still-unadmitted action rechecks current permission and the original allocation;
it cannot silently target the player's new campaign. A different allocation
requires a new explicit request after the old disposition is resolved.

The original actor can retrieve their own historical request outcome after a
membership or permission change. This permits reconciliation, not general access
to the former team's stock, machinery or other members' receipts. Campaign records
referenced by accepted or unresolved work remain identifiable after departure;
leaving does not delete or rename the beneficiary of that work.

Changing work team or campaign never moves, erases or replenishes physical goods.
Explicit gifts remain with their recipient. Team-owned machinery remains where it
is, and revoked access can refuse new operations. The slice uses explicitly owned
or gifted stock; contested property, loans and membership-driven confiscation
need their own policy before being introduced.

Leaving a shared campaign resumes the player's existing solo record. Completed
personal checks and recipes remain. The old shared campaign keeps its accepted
commission credit, which is not copied into the solo record or a new group.
Already accepted allocations survive departure; a later revoked permission does
not retroactively rewrite an accepted physical handoff.

## A continuous six-plate example

These quantities are paper examples, not a new recipe, quota or implemented test.
N is a newcomer working with experienced V in team T. J works with team U.
N deliberately keeps a solo campaign; V and J use their respective team campaigns.

1. Four finite blanks supplied by N become four plates; J supplies two more from
   U's production. N, V and J each carry two plates. There are six actual plates.
2. N confirms an offered two-plate personal check. The same six plates remain in
   their holders. This is possession credit, not six new products or shared study.
3. They physically load one cargo of six. Its allocation is two units to N's solo
   order, two to T's order and two to U's order. Carried stock becomes zero and
   cargo becomes six. No commission has completed yet.
4. The recipient accepts that cargo once. Cargo becomes zero; committed project
   use becomes six, with the three two-unit allocations. The world contains no
   second available copy. N disconnects before seeing the response.
5. On reconnect, N joins U's work team and explicitly opts into U's campaign before
   looking up the old receipt. N does not retry the unresolved handoff or reuse its
   cargo. Reconciliation returns the original receipt: N's solo order still has two,
   T has two and U has two. It does not move N's earlier credit to U or use six
   more plates. N's personal check and recipe entitlements remain unchanged.
6. N later returns to the solo campaign. Its original two units of commission
   credit remain there. T's and U's records remain theirs. No new practice stock
   appears from changing membership or replaying the old request.

If access is revoked before the recipient accepts a pending handoff, that handoff
may be refused and the six units remain with the actual cargo holder. If it was
already accepted, a lost response does not make those consumed units returnable.
The server disposition distinguishes these cases; the client's uncertainty does
not choose one.

## Check the independent-learning cases

| Scenario | Result and practical limit under this experiment |
| --- | --- |
| LP-join | N can keep personal lessons, recipes and a solo campaign while working with T. Actual permitted space, starter parts and inputs must still exist. |
| LP-gift | An explicit gift changes custody once. N may manually confirm an offered inventory check, but gains no automatic study or campaign credit merely by receiving it. |
| LP-machine | N may operate permitted advanced machinery without acquiring its recipe. An earlier method remains a real option only if its site and inputs are available. |
| LP-reveal | T's accepted commission advances T's campaign; N's solo record changes only if allocated cargo. N can defer a message, but cannot hide visible world events. |
| LP-deposit | The exhausted deposit remains empty. A reachable permitted finite source or already accessible alternative supply route is required; exhaustion grants no free unlock. |
| LP-terrain | Excavation persists. The trial must supply an actual safe route and usable practice site under N's current entitlements, rather than a nominal uncompleted lesson. |
| LP-joint | One six-unit cargo can credit several declared beneficiaries only by partitioning those six units. Helpers' recognition does not multiply goods or personal study. |
| LP-switch | Membership may change while a handoff is unresolved. Personal learning and recipes persist; accepted credit stays with its original campaign. Current permission governs new admissions, and a cargo retry waits for reconciliation. |
| LP-return | N sees the current world and may choose a recap or earlier explanation. Personal records do not restore inputs or terrain that changed while N was away. |
| LP-reconnect | Reconcile the original request and current custody before retrying. An accepted receipt cannot acquire a new beneficiary because membership changed. |

The deposit, terrain and return rows are implementation prerequisites, not claims
that a document has solved resource access. Before testing independent learning,
identify the actual authorized site, finite inputs, starter parts and transport
route for N in the already changed world. If none exists, report that case as
unsupported; do not refill a deposit, grant technology because it emptied, or
claim personal counters preserve a playable beginner opportunity.

## Staging and remaining decisions

The next implementation should reuse the existing native World, Session and Store.
It must bind physical debit and credit to one accepted disposition, persist the
declared beneficiary and historical result, and demonstrate refusals, disconnect
reconciliation and no duplicate consumption. This document defines no save schema,
network API, identity service, team UI or off-site transport implementation.

An ordinary inventory check and an off-site commission do not measure sustained
automation. The true ending's products, rates, windows and shared output allocation
need a separate measurement contract; this experiment supplies no ending meter.
World-scale story timing, campaign balance, permanent unlock sharing, property
disputes and guaranteed newcomer resource provision also remain open.

Independent review should reject inconsistent custody, implicit campaign opt-in,
copied historical credit, inaccessible required practice or an accidental change
to the accepted opening. Later executable evidence and actual user feedback may
change this experimental choice. They are not replaced by source review or a
successful paper trace.
