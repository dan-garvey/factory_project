# Terrain composition: next visual pass

This is a revisable proposal for the designed opening site. It is based on passive 1280 × 720 views of the worksite from the east bank, opening orbit and worksite eye at source revision `b14f390` (images in the owning Bead). Those views show a readable grassy construction meadow and distinct fir and broadleaf forms. The far hills still read as smooth continuous slopes; similar small rocks are scattered at nearly even intervals; the river edge has little transition from meadow to water. The player has not playtested these changes.

## Highest value terrain changes

1. **Give the river an erosion story.** Add an asymmetric gravel or cobble bar on the inside of the visible bend, a narrower cut bank opposite it, and two or three partly buried bedrock ribs. Make bank grass taller and darker near wet soil; thin it where the current would scour. This adds shape and material transitions at the strongest current landmark. Keep the waterwheel flow and bank access readable. Flood-scoured bedrock, cobbles and sparse plants are a useful real-world reference: [National Park Service river scour prairies](https://home.nps.gov/biso/learn/nature/cobblebar.htm).
2. **Break the smooth far hills into legible shelves.** One broad shoulder above the meadow, a shallow erosion runnel, and a short exposed-rock cut would make the valley feel formed rather than inflated. Slope silhouettes should remain broad; detail belongs where players see the bank at eye level. Render and physical terrain should use the same height/obstacle description so a visual shortcut does not create hidden barriers or floating footing.
3. **Use clusters and gaps in the vegetation.** Keep the existing species diversity, then place dense shrub/fern patches at moist bank pockets, open grass where the player approaches the ore and builds, and a broken tree line on the higher, drier slope. Several deliberately empty windows across the stream will help orientation and keep the machinery visible. This changes the *distribution* of the existing assets, with little additional source weight.
4. **Make soil follow exposure.** Reuse the current grass and stone maps with low-frequency masks for darker damp soil, small stone-chip fans below exposed rock, and a narrow leaf-litter zone below the broadleaf canopy. The transition should be broad and irregular, not a repeated tiled border. The construction meadow should remain the calmest and clearest surface.
5. **Make ore part of the same geology.** Continue a rock rib into the hill or bank and expose its mineralized fractures only at the actual finite source. Add a sparse apron of angular, mostly buried fragments and restrained oxidized color near those fractures. Quartz or metal-bearing veins following cracks and broken rock are grounded in [USGS descriptions of stockwork and breccia mineralization](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/pressure-gradients-and-boiling-mechanisms-localizing-ore-porphyry-systems). This is a visual reference, not a declaration of the game's mineral system.

## Larger deposit studies

The three `opening/assets/mineral-outcrop/variants/*.tscn` scenes compose different silhouettes from the already credited Boulder 01 scan and its existing stone maps. A retained external Godot renderer captures each packed scene in the same neutral lighting and camera; the images and renderer are linked in `factory-yau.6.45.21.38`. These studies add no gameplay resource, collision, pickup or collector authority. They use scene resources rather than another GDScript because the prototype's staged compiler already has its full 128-script allowance.

| Variant | Visible form | Best prospective use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Faulted ridge | Long low rib with staggered crests and two mineral-stained breaks | A slope-parallel seam where the collector approaches a clear side face |
| River lens | Broad, lower, partly buried mass with a wider apron | A bank-adjacent exposure, only if the water and collector paths remain clear |
| Fractured crown | Compact broad base with a taller broken crest | A distant landmark or later high-reserve source on an upper shelf |

Each spans roughly 7–10 metres in one direction, compared with the current opening rock's 0.51 × 2.28 metre horizontal mesh bounds. These footprints are visual studies, not adopted resource capacities. The first finite source must still be fitted with its collector by `factory-yau.6.45.23.86`; an integrated variant needs one coherent visible/native obstacle, verified cutter and output clearances, traversable approaches, and a separately chosen finite reserve. Decorative copies near the active mine would mislead players about what they can extract, so none are placed in the opening scene by this study.

The same scan is visibly recognizable in some overlapping lobes, especially at close range. Before one becomes a shipped source, make a distinct fracture/vein treatment on the merged form, partly bury its base in authored terrain, and judge it from the player's eye and collector views. The current comparison proves scale and silhouette differences only; it does not establish geology quality, game readability, player understanding or performance.
