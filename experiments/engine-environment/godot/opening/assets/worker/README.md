# Adult worker asset

The complete opening uses **Female_Adult_04** from the
[Microsoft Rocketbox library](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/tree/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9/Assets/Avatars/Adults/Female_Adult_04).
The original publisher releases the model and textures under the included
[MIT license](LICENSE.md); keep that notice with redistributed or modified copies.
The pinned source contains the editable FBX and original textures.
[source.json](source.json) records their identities and preparation settings.
Human feedback still determines whether this revisable character meets the
requested appearance and motion quality.

Named walk/run tracks and the source rest pose feed the character presenter.
The confirmed actor supplies position and motion; visual feet, hands and the
multitool do not grant reach or replace actor collision.

The first-person tool view combines a posed MakeHuman Community MPFB 2.0.18
right hand with a cropped sleeve from the pinned Rocketbox worker. The MPFB
system asset metadata identifies its base and skin resources as CC0. The hand
is posed around the existing cutter handle; the world-view cutter remains on
the worker's hand bone. [source.json](source.json) records both baked mesh
identities and their conversion inputs. The Rocketbox sleeve retains its MIT
notice in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

[source.json](source.json) retains exact conversion inputs and output identities.

views do not establish human appearance quality or natural motion in every view.
