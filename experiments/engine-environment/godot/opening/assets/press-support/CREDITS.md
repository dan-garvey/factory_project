# Cast press supports

The column, cast mounting collar and fasteners are adapted from **Drill Press 01**,
by **Viktor**, published by [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/drill_press_01)
under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Attribution is retained for provenance.
The provider's website preview is not shipped.

Factory reuses the selected component twice to support the ordinary processor.
The original plinth, bridge height, anvil, guides, moving head, piston and outlet
retain their work coordinates. A source-derived mounting plane meets the plinth;
the column top meets the bridge. Lower fasteners retain their small penetration
into that visual plinth. The right copy turns by half a revolution, preserving
the shape and atlas. The complete donor base, table, motor housing, controls,
belt, pulleys, chuck and electrical details are outside this component.

The selected source shape, separate normal/UV records and triangle order remain.
One uniform scale fits the existing 1.16-metre span above the mounting plane.
These are revisable adapter fitting values, not additional gameplay dimensions
or a structural load calculation. The bridge's olive paint is a matching art
choice; historical rosters retain their original paint and rectangular supports.

The albedo remains the original one-kilopixel JPEG. Linear OpenGL normal and
packed occlusion/roughness/metallic maps use floating-point Lanczos sampling,
normal renormalization, nearest RGB8 encoding and lossless WebP at 512 pixels.
Lossless delivery preserves those prepared pixels, not every original spatial
detail. No channel reorder, green-axis flip or gamma conversion is applied.
Source UVs, double-sided shading and cached mipmaps remain active.

[source.json](source.json) records the exact inputs and delivered hashes.
The [offline recipe](../../../../../../native/asset-preparation/README.md)
describes extraction and fitting. The same mesh and material serve both supports
and every placed processor. Source meshes create no collider, controller,
mechanical connection, quantity or state owner. The native launcher captures raw
source assets; packaged Godot exports remain unverified. Resource and visual-fit
checks do not establish rendering quality, native physical contact, GPU cost or
human acceptance.
