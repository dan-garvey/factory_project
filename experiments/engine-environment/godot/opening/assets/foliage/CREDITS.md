# Grass tufts

[Grass Medium 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01), modeled by
**Rico Cilliers** with photography by **Rob Tuytel**, is supplied by Poly Haven
under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). This package redistributes the selected
asset and transformed source textures, not the provider's website or example
renders. Original downloads, metadata and results are retained externally in
`sourced-worksite-foliage-20261021`; [source.json](source.json) records their pins.

Only the compact `grass_medium_01_mid_b_LOD0` variant is included: 1,386 vertices,
1,257 triangles and one material. The source's full catalogue headline is not
this mesh's triangle count. Source-local roots and geometry are preserved with
a reversible uniform scale of two; the unrelated catalogue placement is omitted.
Its resulting uninstanced height is about 0.355 metres. Existing terrain anchors,
rotations and scale variations place the sparse outer tufts and continuous meadow
patches around the natural worksite, clear of the river and constructed volumes.
They remain noncolliding scenery, with no independent script or clock.

The photographed color is combined with the provider's separate opacity map,
then resized to 384 pixels. OpenGL normal and packed linear occlusion/roughness/
metallic maps use 256 pixels. The source's zero metallic factor is preserved.
Lossless embedded PNGs retain prepared bytes. A double-sided cutout at 0.5 keeps
spaces between blades. Cached color mips retain the nearest achievable source
cutout coverage and dilate color into transparent borders; normal mips retain
renormalized vectors. The original mesh and embedded map bytes are unchanged.
Near-neutral instance tint avoids multiplying the photograph by the former dark
generated-blade colors. The current grass material derives a muted green palette
from the source luminance detail, preserving every base-opacity byte, and uses a
lower dielectric specular response. This replaces the photograph's dark bands
and dry coloration as a reversible meadow art choice; it is not a change to the
licensed source files. The shared fern importer keeps its own color and finish.
Two-sided shadow participation is presentation only.

[Native packaging instructions](../../../../../../native/foliage-assets/README.md)
describe the reproducible selection and image processing. The ordinary buffer
import requires no editor cache or external texture files. Historical fixed
rosters without the asset retain their explicit generated foliage fallback.
A present invalid package reports its status rather than claiming source import.
Geometry/resource tests are not human visual or Satisfactory-parity evidence.
