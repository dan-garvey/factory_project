# Broadleaf scenery

The two scenic broadleaf trees use Poly Haven's [Island Tree 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_02), scanned and processed by Rob Tuytel and Rico Cilliers, under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The source was downloaded on 2026-10-23. [source.json](source.json) records the official one-kilopixel glTF, geometry and nine texture URLs, sizes, provider checksums and local SHA-256 checksums. [prepare_maps.gd](../foliage/prepare_maps.gd) reproduces the selected source preparation; the complete original source bundle and reports are retained with the local asset study.

The source has separate trunk, modeled foliage and branch surfaces. Offline Godot 4.7.2 preparation selects 6,824 trunk, 275,674 foliage and 39,400 branch triangles, then compacts the selected vertex buffers. The color maps are prepared at 192 pixels and normal and packed material maps at 96 pixels, with mipmaps. The three surfaces and maps are bundled in [broadleaf_tree.res](broadleaf_tree.res), a self-contained 6,350,541-byte resource with SHA-256 `f60777631631d6510df34e8b0ab56b62e6ab99b664aa2246b1fa57085bc996ad`. A fresh preparation may serialize different resource bytes; this hash identifies the shipped file.

The ordinary scene mounts each complete tree once. The provider's pale scan base is seated below the local soil. Both trees and their exact transformed trunk triangles use the same terrain-rooted pose for presentation and static physical contact. Their full crowns clear the river, fixed rocks and construction area. Foliage and branches are visual; these trees are not a new harvestable source or inventory state.

The matching passive eye-height and elevated views show a fuller, more distinctive silhouette than the preceding Tree Small 02 resource.
