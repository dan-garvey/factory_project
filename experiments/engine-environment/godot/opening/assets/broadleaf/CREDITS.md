# Broadleaf trees

The distinct broadleaf tree derives from Poly Haven's [Tree Small 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02), by Rico Cilliers, under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The original one-kilopixel glTF, binary geometry and maps were downloaded on 2026-10-22. The pinned source hashes are in [prepare_maps.gd](../foliage/prepare_maps.gd) and [source.json](source.json); retained preparation reports live outside the repository. `broadleaf_tree.res` has SHA-256 `71b06d2f9252ca64f9d4bcfda6bcaf0db1fd2c67fdbd7d4c77bce810f913113f` and no external resource dependencies.

The source leaf mesh has 1,939,380 triangles. The pinned Godot 4.7.2 importer selects bounded generated distance levels for branches, leaves and trunk with 11,372, 49,120 and 7,071 triangles respectively. The selected geometry is both displayed and physical; there is no hidden box or card impostor. Its separate source opacity map is packed into the prepared leaf color as a two-sided cutout. Prepared color uses 192-pixel mipmaps; normal and packed material maps use 128-pixel mipmaps. The resource is 1,836,428 bytes.

Two rooted trees are placed outside the working ring, river and rock envelopes. They share the site surface's deterministic positions and contribute the actual transformed 7,071 trunk triangles per tree to `PhysicalGround`. Their canopy is visual foliage. These trees are scenery, not a new harvestable wood source or inventory state. Resource roundtrip, placement, native capsule contact and passive site images were checked; ordinary play, human visual preference and frame cost remain unmeasured.
